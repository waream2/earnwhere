MyHotelReports is a hospitality reporting platform for operators who need clearer month-end communication with property owners. It supports owner statements, configurable PDF reports, owner messaging, reservation visibility, and backend-gated access to financial and operational data.

The product work lives in the unglamorous parts of the workflow: getting statement data into a shape that can be reviewed, rendered, corrected, and delivered; making access boundaries explicit; and giving operators a system that can survive repeated monthly use instead of a one-off export ritual.
